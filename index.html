shorts-uploader

A private tool that uploads 3D animated explainer Shorts, made by the owner of the T.A Products YouTube channel, to that channel.

Uses ▶ YouTube API Services

How it works

  1. The channel owner signs in with Google and grants permission to upload videos.
  2. The tool uploads finished videos, with title, description and tags, to the owner's own YouTube channel.
  3. The sign-in token stays on the owner's computer. No other user's data is accessed.
