Privacy Policy

App: shorts-uploader
Last updated: 15 November 2026

shorts-uploader is a private tool used by the owner of the T.A Products YouTube channel to upload videos they have created to their own channel. It is not offered to the public.

What data the app accesses

With your permission through Google's sign-in, the app uses the YouTube Data API to:

  • upload videos to your YouTube channel (youtube.upload), and
  • read your channel's name and basic statistics, to confirm the right channel is connected (youtube.readonly).

How data is stored

The sign-in token Google issues is stored only on the owner's own computer. It is never sent to any server other than Google's. The app has no database, no analytics and no third-party trackers.

Sharing

The app does not sell, share or transfer any Google user data to anyone. Data obtained through Google APIs is used only to upload and manage videos on the connected channel.

Google API Services User Data Policy

This app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. It also uses YouTube API Services; see the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

You can remove the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the local token file also stops all access.
